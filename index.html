ABOUT

Useful learning.
Thoughtful privacy.

I am a Lecturer in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Tongling University. My research focuses on privacy protection and federated learning: how to learn from distributed data while protecting individuals and keeping collaborative systems reliable.

I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology in June 2025, and joined Tongling University in July 2025.

My work spans local differential privacy, poisoning defenses, efficient learning for connected devices, and the security of language model applications.

PRIVACY-PRESERVING LEARNING

FDLDP
Private pre-trainingFederated fine-tuning

Computer Networks · 2026

A better starting point for private learning.

A two-stage framework uses locally private reports to initialize a global model, then protects client updates during federated fine-tuning. The work studies the connection between privacy, convergence and communication.

Read the paper↗

ROBUST FEDERATED LEARNING

FedCleaner
Anomaly detectionMachine unlearning

ProvSec 2025 · 2026

Detect, then remove harmful influence.

A server-side defense combines proactive layer-wise anomaly detection with retroactive contribution erasure to address poisoned updates and their influence on the global model.

Read the paper↗

LLM SECURITY

Prompt-in-Content
Uploaded inputsOutput hijacking

NSS 2025 · 2026

When uploaded content becomes an instruction.

This study examines adversarial prompts embedded in otherwise ordinary inputs, how they redirect model outputs, and why content isolation matters for safer LLM applications.
